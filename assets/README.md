# README illustrations

Both illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. They show unbranded concept hardware. The WebP files preserve the original compositions.

## Scanner and NAS

File [scanner-on-the-nas.webp](scanner-on-the-nas.webp).

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: wide cover image for the README of a small open-source home scanner project
Primary request: a polished, photorealistic product photograph of a compact Wi-Fi sheet-fed document scanner ready to digitize a letter at home.
Scene: a calm warm-white studio backdrop and off-white desk, with faint physical surface texture and an unobtrusive soft horizon. The only subjects are the scanner, a sheet of paper, and a small two-bay NAS behind it.
Subject: a slim matte-graphite portable scanner with a low rectangular silhouette about 7 times wider than tall, subtle top-panel seams, a front paper-feed slot and one tiny cool-blue ready light. One white sheet lies at a shallow angle with its leading edge visibly entering the front slot. A small dark two-bay NAS sits behind and to the side, secondary and slightly softer in focus. Generic unbranded hardware.
Composition: wide horizontal 2.4:1 cover composition, three-quarter desk-level view. Keep the entire scanner and the paper comfortably inside the middle height of the frame so the wide image has breathing room. Clear focus on the scanner and paper, with generous margins and no typography overlay.
Style: restrained Apple Design-inspired industrial product photography. Use visual hierarchy, real matte material, soft directional light, subtle depth and deliberate spacing. Palette of warm porcelain, graphite and one muted blue detail. No people, branding, text, legible document marks, screens, cables or watermarks.
```

## Paper to PDF

File [paper-to-pdf.webp](paper-to-pdf.webp).

```text
Use case: productivity-visual
Asset type: wide editorial illustration for a GitHub README explaining the path from paper to a searchable PDF
Primary request: show three physical objects from left to right in a single seamless composition: a portable scanner feeding a sheet, a small NAS that receives the scan, and two overlapping white document pages representing one duplex PDF. Use a subtle hairline connection between the three to establish the direction of the flow.
Scene: the same warm porcelain background and soft desk plane as a premium studio product shoot. The three objects are equally spaced, oriented in a consistent slight three-quarter view, with aligned optical centres and clear visual separation.
Style: precise Apple Design-inspired product illustration with restrained depth and realistic materials, a low matte-graphite scanner bar, a small graphite two-bay NAS, and white sheets supported by one softly translucent pale-blue glass tile. Soft cast shadows give each item its place. Faint abstract strokes may indicate the document surface; they must not resemble legible text.
Composition: wide 2.6:1 landscape composition for a narrow section in a README, all objects complete and comfortably within the central band, with substantial negative space around the outside. The first two objects are recognisable at small sizes. No extra objects, people, logos, typography, words, digits, borders or watermarks.
```
